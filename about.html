Our Story

SHALVADZE started from something simple: knowing how business in China actually works on the ground.

We want sourcing from China to feel approachable, whether you're an established company, a small business, or simply taking your first steps into sourcing. You don't need to be a big buyer to start a conversation with us.

Finding a product is easy. Finding the right supplier, asking the right questions and making sure everything goes as expected is where the real work begins.

That’s why I started SHALVADZE — to give businesses a reliable point of contact closer to the suppliers, factories and markets they work with.

No complicated promises. Just practical support, clear communication and a genuine effort to get things right.
